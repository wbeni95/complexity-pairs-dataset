# Licensing and commercial use

This research is independent and needs funding. The dataset and code are free for non-commercial
use. Commercial use requires a paid license, and the fees fund the research.

## Free: no permission needed

- **Personal use**: study, research, experiments and hobby projects with no anticipated commercial application.
- **Education**: schools, universities, courses, textbooks used in teaching.
- **Non-profit and public research**: universities, public research organisations, charities, and government institutions.

Attribution is required. Cite the dataset and link to this repository.

## Requires a commercial license

- Any use by or for a for-profit company, including internal research and development.
- **Training, fine-tuning or evaluating commercial machine-learning models** on the dataset, or on data derived from it.
- Building products or services, paid or free, that incorporate the code, the entries, or derivatives of them.
- Redistributing the dataset or code as part of a commercial offering.

To obtain a commercial license, contact the maintainer via GitHub ([@wbeni95](https://github.com/wbeni95)).
Terms are negotiated per use case, and research-stage startups and academic spin-offs are welcome to ask.

## What the licenses cover, and what they don't

| Part of the repository | License |
|---|---|
| Entries (`entry.json`), READMEs, notes, `index.json`, and the curated compilation as a whole | [CC BY-NC 4.0](LICENSE) |
| Code: `tools/`, `lib/`, harnesses, implementations, tests | [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE-CODE) |

The licenses cover **this repository's own expression**: its code, text, curated data, verification results,
and the selection and arrangement of the collection (in the EU, the compilation may also be protected by
database rights). They do **not** restrict mathematical facts, published algorithms, or results from the
cited literature. Anyone may reimplement an algorithm from its original paper.
